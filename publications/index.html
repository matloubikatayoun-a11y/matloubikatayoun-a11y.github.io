The Persistence of the Void: Formal Absence in Holocaust Representation from Langfus to Ida

Katayoun Matloubi

DOI: 10.1080/17504902.2026.2742079

Argues against the assumption that formal absence is inherently ethical in Holocaust representation. Reads Langfus, Shoah, Son of Saul, and Ida asymmetrically, as a genealogy of mediation.
